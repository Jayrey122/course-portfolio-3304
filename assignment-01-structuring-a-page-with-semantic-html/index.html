My Top 5 TV Shows

Assignment 01: Semantic HTML Top 5 List

  1. Vampire Diaries

    Vampire Diaries is one of my favorite shows because it has drama, romance, and supernatural stuff all in one. I like how the show follows Elena and the Salvatore brothers and there is always some kind of twist that makes you want to keep watching. Even though it can get crazy sometimes. The characters and their relationships are what make the show good.

  2. Criminal Minds

    Criminal Minds is another show I really like because it focuses on the FBI's Behavioral Analysis Unit and how they figure out criminals. I enjoy trying to understand why people do the things they do so this show is interesting to me. Each episode has a different case, but the team members and their personalitiesare what keep me coming back to it.

  3. The Summer I Turned Pretty

    The Summer I Turned Pretty is a good show if you like romance and summer vibes. I like how it follows Belly and her relationships with the Fisher brothers especially because there is always drama between them. It is one of those shows that is easy to get invested in because you start picking sides and wanting to know what happens next.

  4. Brooklynn Nine-Nine

    Brooklyn Nine-Nine is one of my favorite comedies because it is funnywithout trying too hard. I like how each detective has a totally different personality, and together they make the show entertaining. Even though it is about police work it is mostly lightharted and easy to watch when I just want something funny.

  5. Stranger Things

    Stranger Things is a show I like because it mixes sci-fi, horror, and friendship. I enjoy how the kids work together to figure out what is happening in Hawkins and the Upside Down makes the show feel mysterious and intense. It also has good characters so it is easy to get attached to them and want to see how their stories end.
